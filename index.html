WHAT IS STUDY HALL?

Study Hall is a simple starting place for your school routine. Its calm layout is made to help you settle in, think about what you want to work on, and take a short reset between classes or assignments. Keep your day moving one step at a time, then use the Homework button when you are ready to continue to your personal portal.
